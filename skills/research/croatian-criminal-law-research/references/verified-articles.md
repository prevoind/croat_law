## Non-KB law used alongside (general knowledge — verify if cited formally)
- Ustav RH: Art. 28 presumption of innocence (mirrors ZKP 3), Art. 35 privacy/dignity, Art. 38 freedom of expression.
- ECHR Art. 6(2) case law: *Allenet de Ribemont v. France* (official's pre-conviction guilt statement = violation + damages), *Daktaras*, *Garycki* (whole-statement-in-context test); "suspects arrested" framing usually safer than asserting guilt.
- GDPR Art. 9 (biometric: faces) / Art. 10 (criminal-offense data) — publishing identifiable suspect footage to a public platform is processing beyond law-enforcement purpose; MUP is controller.
- zakon.hr IDs (search via `https://www.zakon.hr/search?q=...`): EMA = /z/196/, ZPPO = /z/173/. Verify page <title> before trusting a /z/<id>/ URL.
